| C-03 | `src/namma/recon/io.read_colmap_images_txt` (Phase 12) | Filters empty lines, so an image with **zero** 2-D points would shift every following record. | Unit test `tests/test_p13_recon.py::test_colmap_images_txt_handles_images_without_points` (new parser `namma.recon.colmap.read_images_txt`). | No effect on Phase 12 numbers: every registered kicker image had observations (30 matched poses were evaluated).
