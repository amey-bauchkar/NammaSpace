Scanning tips

  • Walk, don't spin. Move along the walls, 2–3 small steps between looks. Coverage rises when you look at a part of the room again from another spot.
  • Move slowly and smoothly; keep the phone upright.
  • Show every wall, the floor and the furniture from about 1–2 m.
  • Turn the lights on; avoid pointing straight at windows or lamps.
  • When the walls are covered, tap Done – the computer does the rest.
